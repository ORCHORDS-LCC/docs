# Data Governance and AI Ethics

## Overview

Data governance establishes policies, standards, and procedures for managing data as a strategic asset. AI ethics ensures artificial intelligence systems operate transparently, fairly, and responsibly.

## Data Governance Framework

### 1. Data Stewardship
- **Data Owners**: Executive sponsors responsible for data domains
- **Data Stewards**: Subject matter experts managing data quality
- **Data Custodians**: Technical teams maintaining data infrastructure
- **Data Users**: Individuals accessing data for business purposes

### 2. Data Quality Management
- Define quality dimensions: accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness
- Establish data quality rules and validation checks
- Monitor data quality metrics continuously
- Implement corrective actions for quality violations

### 3. Data Lifecycle Management
- **Creation**: Standards for data entry and capture
- **Storage**: Classification, retention, and archival policies
- **Usage**: Access controls, purpose limitation, audit trails
- **Disposal**: Secure deletion and certification of destruction

### 4. Data Privacy and Protection
- Comply with GDPR, CCPA, and regional privacy regulations
- Implement data minimization principles
- Establish consent management frameworks
- Maintain data processing records and impact assessments

## AI Ethics Principles

### 1. Fairness
- Audit algorithms for bias across demographic groups
- Implement fairness constraints in model training
- Monitor for disparate impact in production
- Establish redress mechanisms for unfair outcomes

### 2. Transparency
- Document model purpose, data sources, and limitations
- Provide explainable AI outputs for high-stakes decisions
- Maintain model cards and datasheets for datasets
- Disclose AI system capabilities and constraints to users

### 3. Accountability
- Establish clear responsibility for AI system outcomes
- Implement human oversight for critical decisions
- Create audit trails for model training and deployment
- Define escalation procedures for AI-related incidents

### 4. Privacy by Design
- Minimize data collection to essential requirements
- Implement differential privacy techniques where applicable
- Anonymize or pseudonymize personal data
- Conduct privacy impact assessments for AI projects

## Governance Structure

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Data Governance Council | Strategic oversight and policy approval |
| Chief Data Officer | Executive accountability for data strategy |
| AI Ethics Board | Review and approve AI system deployments |
| Data Stewards | Day-to-day data quality and compliance |
| AI Engineers | Ethical implementation of models |

## Compliance Requirements

- **GDPR**: EU data protection regulation
- **CCPA/CPRA**: California consumer privacy act
- **AI Act**: EU artificial intelligence regulation (risk-based)
- **Sector-specific**: HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOX as applicable
