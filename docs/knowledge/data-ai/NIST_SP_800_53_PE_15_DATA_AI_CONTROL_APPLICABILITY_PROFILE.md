# Water Damage Protection Data AI Control Applicability Profile

## Reference

- Control: **pe-15 — Water Damage Protection**
- Family: pe

## Control statement



## Data and AI applicability review

1. Identify the data pipeline, model, training or inference workload, or AI-facing service in scope.
2. Decide whether the control applies, and record the reason.
3. Resolve the organization-defined parameters, noting any that are specific to data or AI workloads.
4. Review the control against the system's actual behaviour.
5. Record the evidence that would demonstrate the control for a data or AI context.
6. Record the gaps, their owners, and the remediation dates.

## Evidence expectations

- the data and AI system inventory for the scope;
- the applicability decision per system;
- the evidence reviewed, with its date;
- the gap list with owners and dates.

## Limitations

This review is derived from the stated control. It does not claim the control is satisfied in any data or AI system, and it does not replace the assessment evidence.
