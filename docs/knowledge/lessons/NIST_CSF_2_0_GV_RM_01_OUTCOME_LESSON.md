# Risk management objectives are established and agreed to by organizational stakeholders Outcome Lesson

## Authoritative outcome

**GV.RM-01:** Risk management objectives are established and agreed to by organizational stakeholders

## Reusable lesson

This outcome should be treated as an explicit review question when it is relevant to the system or organisation, rather than assumed to be satisfied by tooling, policy language, or another control.

## Apply the lesson

1. Define the real scope before judging the outcome.
2. Inspect current technical, process, governance, or operational evidence.
3. Separate documented intent from verified behaviour.
4. Record partial coverage and unresolved gaps instead of converting them into a pass.
5. Revisit the outcome when the underlying scope, threat environment, technology, suppliers, or mission materially changes.
