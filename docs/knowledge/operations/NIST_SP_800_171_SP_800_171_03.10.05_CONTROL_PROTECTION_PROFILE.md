# 03.10.05 Control Protection Profile

## Reference

- Control: **SP_800_171_03.10.05 — 03.10.05**
- Family: SP_800_171_03.10

## Control statement



## Protection profile

1. Confirm the control applies to the systems in scope, and record the applicability decision.
2. Resolve the organization-defined parameters before evaluating the requirement.
3. Identify the protected asset or process the control operates on.
4. Record the implementation evidence and the operational records that show the control operating over time.
5. Record the supplier or inherited responsibility where the protection is shared.
6. Review when the catalog is revised or when the protected asset changes materially.

## Evidence expectations

- the resolved statement and parameters, dated and attributable;
- design and configuration evidence for the protected asset;
- operational records showing the control operating;
- supplier evidence where the protection is inherited.

## Limitations

The statement describes a general requirement. Whether it is met is a property of the implementation, established only by assessment evidence.
