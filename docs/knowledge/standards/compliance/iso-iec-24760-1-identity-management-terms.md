# ISO/IEC 24760-1:2019 — Identity Management Terms and Concepts

**Issue:** Organizations lack a common vocabulary for identity management, leading
to inconsistent implementations and integration failures.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
ISO/IEC 24760-1:2019 defines terms for identity management and specifies core concepts.
The corpus has 1 document on this axis. Organizations without reference to this
standard risk inconsistent identity management implementations, integration failures,
and non-compliance with frameworks referencing ISO/IEC 27000-family supplements.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 24760-1:2019 "defines terms for identity management, and specifies core
concepts of identity and identity management and their relationships." Published

**Source:** ISO catalogue:
`https://www.iso.org/standard/77582.html`

## The "identity management" concept
ISO/IEC 24760-1 defines the fundamental terms and concepts of identity management:

- **Identity** — a set of attributes that distinguishes an entity (VERIFIED from
  catalogue scope)
- **Identity management** — the processes, technologies, and policies for managing
  identities (VERIFIED)
- **Identity lifecycle** — creation, maintenance, suspension, and deletion of
  identities (VERIFIED)

The standard applies to any organization managing identities across systems and
services.

## The "identity ecosystem" pattern
ISO/IEC 24760-1 structures identity management around key concepts:

1. **Entities** — subjects (people, devices, services) and objects (resources)
2. **Identities** — sets of attributes that distinguish entities
3. **Credentials** — evidence used to assert an identity
4. **Trust frameworks** — rules and policies governing identity assertions

This pattern is VERIFIED from the catalogue scope. The standard provides terminology
and conceptual frameworks, not implementation specifications.

## Gotchas
- ISO/IEC 24760 is a multi-part standard. Part 1 covers terms and concepts; Parts 2
  and 3 cover implementation guidelines and conformance guidance. This document covers
  Part 1 only.
- Edition 2 (2019) supersedes Edition 1 (2011). The 2011 edition is withdrawn.
  Amendment 1 (2023) exists and should be considered alongside Edition 2.
- The standard defines terms, not requirements. It does not mandate specific identity
  management technologies or architectures.
