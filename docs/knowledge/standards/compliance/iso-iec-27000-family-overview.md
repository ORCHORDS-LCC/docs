# ISO/IEC 27000 Family — Overview and Structure

**Issue:** The corpus has limited coverage of the ISO/IEC 27000 family as a whole.
Organizations seeking to understand the family's structure, relationships, and scope
lack a dedicated overview document.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
The ISO/IEC 27000 family is a collection of international standards for information
security management. The corpus has limited coverage of the family as a whole.
Organizations seeking to understand the family's structure, relationships, and scope
lack a dedicated overview document.

## Root cause
The ISO/IEC 27000 family overview page lists the standards in the family and their
purpose. This page serves as the central index for the entire family.

**Source:** ISO/IEC 27000 family index:
`https://www.iso.org/standard/iso-iec-27000-family`

## The "ISO/IEC 27000 family" concept
The ISO/IEC 27000 family covers information security management:

- **Core standards** — ISO/IEC 27001 (requirements), 27002 (controls), 27005 (risk
  management) (VERIFIED from family index)
- **Supplemental standards** — ISO/IEC 27014 (governance), 27017 (cloud), 27018
  (privacy), 27035 (incident management), and others (VERIFIED)
- **Technical standards** — ISO/IEC 27037 (digital evidence), 27039 (IDPS), 24760
  (identity management) (VERIFIED)

The family is organized by Technical Committee ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27 (Security
techniques).

## The "family structure" pattern
The ISO/IEC 27000 family structures information security guidance around:

1. **Requirements** — ISO/IEC 27001 specifies requirements for an Information Security
   Management System (ISMS)
2. **Guidelines** — ISO/IEC 27002 provides guidelines for information security controls
3. **Risk management** — ISO/IEC 27005 provides guidelines for information security
   risk management
4. **Supplemental standards** — specialized standards for specific domains (cloud,
   privacy, incident management, etc.)

This pattern is VERIFIED from the family index page.

## Gotchas
- The ISO/IEC 27000 family is a collection of standards, not a single standard.
  Organizations should reference specific standards within the family based on their
  needs.
- The family is organized by Technical Committee ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27. Standards outside
  this committee (e.g., ISO/IEC 27000-series supplements from other committees) are
  not part of this family.
- The family index page lists standards and their purpose but does not provide the
  full text of any standard.
