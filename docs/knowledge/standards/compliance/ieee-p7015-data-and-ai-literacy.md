## The "data and AI literacy" concept
IEEE P7015 establishes an operational framework and associated capabilities to coordinate global data and AI literacy building efforts. It provides a framework for assessing and improving data and AI literacy at organizational and individual levels.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE P7015 (project, no year)
- **Title:** Standard for Data and Artificial Intelligence (AI) Literacy, Skills, and Readiness
- **Status:** Project (P = proposed, under development)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "AI literacy" pattern
The project provides a framework for data and AI literacy:
- Defining core competencies for data and AI literacy
- Establishing operational frameworks for literacy building
- Assessing organizational readiness for AI adoption
- Creating training and education programs
- Evaluating literacy outcomes and improvements

This pattern is critical for:
- Workforce development and reskilling
- Organizational AI readiness assessments
- Educational program design
- Regulatory compliance for AI literacy requirements

## Gotchas
- IEEE P7015 is a *project* (P = proposed, under development), not a published standard — there is no year.
- The project applies to all organizations and individuals, not just technology companies — though technology organizations are a primary use case.
- The full project text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
