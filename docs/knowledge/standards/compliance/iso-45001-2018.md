# ISO 45001:2018 — Occupational health and safety management systems — Requirements with guidance for use

## Issue

The corpus has no document on ISO 45001, the International Standard for occupational health and safety management systems — requirements with guidance for use.

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom

An organisation runs information security and business continuity programmes but has no reference document describing ISO 45001, the standard that sets out requirements for an occupational health and safety (OH&S) management system, including the guidance clauses that help organisations implement it effectively.

## Root cause

ISO 45001:2018 specifies requirements and provides guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining and improving an occupational health and safety management system. It is intended to be usable by any organisation, regardless of size, type and nature, and applies to all hazards relevant to the organisation's OH&S risks. The standard follows the same high-level structure (Annex SL) as ISO 14001 and ISO 9001, enabling integrated management systems.

Edition 1 was published in March 2018, replacing the withdrawn ISO/TS 45001:2014 technical specification. The standard is currently in stage 90.92 (to be revised), meaning a future revision is planned but none has been published yet.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/63787.html

## The "occupational health and safety" concept

ISO 45001:2018 is titled "Occupational health and safety management systems — Requirements with guidance for use." It is the first ISO standard to address occupational health and safety directly with a certifiable requirements specification. The standard covers the full PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle and incorporates guidance throughout its clauses — notably in sub-clause 6.1.2.2, which requires organisations to consider the perspectives and needs of workers and other relevant interested parties when identifying hazards and assessing OH&S risks.

The standard is structured to integrate with other management system standards through Annex SL, sharing the same ten core clauses: context of the organisation, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement. This enables organisations to combine ISO 45001 with ISO 9001 (quality) and ISO 14001 (environment) into a single integrated management system.

A key differentiator of ISO 45001 is its emphasis on **worker participation and consultation** — a requirement that did not appear in the earlier ISO/TS 45001:2014 technical specification. The standard requires organisations to provide non-blocking, non-penalised mechanisms for workers at all levels to participate in decision-making about OH&S matters.

## Verification

- ISO 45001:2018 — Published, Edition 1, 2018-03, 41 pages. Catalogue page: `https://www.iso.org/standard/63787.html`
- Stage 90.92 — International Standard to be revised. A future revision is planned but not yet published.
- Technical Committee: ISO/TC 283 (Occupational health and safety management). ICS: 13.100 (Occupational safety. Industrial hygiene), 03.100.70 (Management systems).

The standard includes an amendment: ISO 45001:2018/Amd 1:2024, which provides additional content available for purchase but not included in the base standard text.

## Gotchas

- **Stage 90.92 (to be revised)** does not mean withdrawn — it means a revision is planned. The standard remains valid and certifiable as-is.
- **ISO/TS 45001:2014 was a technical specification, not a standard.** It was withdrawn when ISO 45001:2018 was published. Do not cite the TS number for current work.
- **The standard includes guidance within its clauses.** Unlike many ISO standards that separate requirements from guidance, ISO 45001 embeds guidance text directly in the normative clauses (marked with "note" and "guidance" labels).
- **Worker participation is a core requirement.** Clause 5.4 specifically requires organisations to provide mechanisms for workers at all levels to participate in the OH&S management system. This is not optional guidance — it is a certifiable requirement.
- **It covers both "health" and "safety."** The standard's scope extends beyond physical safety to include psychological health, work-related stress, and organisational culture factors that affect worker wellbeing.
