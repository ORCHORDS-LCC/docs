## The "ethically driven nudging" concept
IEEE P7008 defines 'nudges' as exhibited by robotic, intelligent or autonomous systems as overt or hidden suggestions or manipulations designed to influence the autonomy or decision-making of a person. It provides a framework for understanding and regulating nudging behavior in autonomous systems.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE P7008 (project, no year)
- **Title:** Standard for Ethically Driven Nudging for Robotic, Intelligent and Autonomous Systems
- **Status:** Project (P = proposed, under development)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "nudge" pattern
The project provides a framework for understanding ethically driven nudging:
- Defining what constitutes a "nudge" in autonomous systems
- Distinguishing overt from hidden nudges
- Evaluating the ethical implications of nudging behavior
- Establishing guidelines for ethical nudging design
- Assessing the impact of nudges on human autonomy

This pattern is critical for:
- Consumer protection and autonomy preservation
- Regulatory compliance for autonomous systems
- Ethical AI design principles
- User trust and transparency

## Gotchas
- IEEE P7008 is a *project* (P = proposed, under development), not a published standard — there is no year.
- The project applies to all robotic, intelligent and autonomous systems, not just AI — though AI/ML systems are a primary use case.
- The full project text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
