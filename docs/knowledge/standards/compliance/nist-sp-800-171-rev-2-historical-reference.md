# NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 — Protecting CUI (Historical Reference)

**Issue:** The corpus has 4 documents on NIST SP 800-171 but none specifically covering
Rev. 2, the previous version superseded by Rev. 3 in May 2024. Organizations
transitioning from Rev. 2 to Rev. 3 need a reference for understanding changes.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 was published February 2020 and superseded by Rev. 3 in May
2024. The corpus has 4 documents on NIST SP 800-171 but none specifically covering
Rev. 2 as a historical reference. Organizations transitioning from Rev. 2 to Rev. 3
need context on the previous version's scope and applicability.

## Root cause
NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2, "Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information in Nonfederal
Systems and Organizations", was published February 2020. Authors: Ron Ross (NIST),
Victoria Pillitteri (NIST), Kelley Dempsey (NIST), Mark Riddle (NARA), Gary Guissanie
(IDA).
The publication "provides agencies with recommended security requirements for protecting
the confidentiality of CUI when the information is resident in nonfederal systems and
organizations; when the nonfederal organization is not collecting or maintaining
information on behalf of a federal agency or using or operating a system on behalf of
an agency; and where there are no specific safeguarding requirements for protecting the
confidentiality of CUI prescribed by the authorizing law, regulation, or governmentwide
policy for the CUI category listed in the CUI Registry."

**Source:** NIST CSRC:
`https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/171/r2/final`

## The "CUI protection" concept (Rev. 2)
NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 defined security requirements for protecting CUI:

- **CUI protection requirements** — security requirements for protecting CUI in
  nonfederal systems (VERIFIED from publication abstract)
- **Applicability** — applies to all components of nonfederal systems that process,
  store, and/or transmit CUI (VERIFIED)
- **Contractual use** — intended for use by federal agencies in contractual vehicles
  or other agreements (VERIFIED)

The publication applied to organizations where there were no specific safeguarding
requirements prescribed by the authorizing law, regulation, or governmentwide policy
for the CUI category listed in the CUI Registry.

## The "superseded revision" pattern
NIST SP 800-171 revisions evolved:

1. **Original publication** — Initial CUI protection requirements
2. **Rev. 1 (2015)** — Updated for Executive Order 13556
3. **Rev. 2 (2020)** — Current version at time of publication, superseded by Rev. 3
   (May 2024)
4. **Rev. 3 (2024)** — Latest version, incorporating updated requirements

This evolution is VERIFIED from the CSRC listing showing "Supersedes: SP 800-171 Rev. 2
(01/28/2021)" for Rev. 3.

## Gotchas
- NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 is superseded by Rev. 3 (May 2024). Organizations should
  reference Rev. 3 for current CUI protection requirements.
- This document serves as a historical reference for organizations transitioning from
  Rev. 2 to Rev. 3. It does not represent current requirements.
- The publication date is February 2020; the CSRC listing shows "01/28/2021" as the
  superseded date for Rev. 3, which may reflect a formatting difference in the CSRC
  listing.
