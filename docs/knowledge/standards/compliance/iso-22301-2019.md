# ISO 22301:2019 — Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems — Requirements

**Issue:** The corpus has no document on ISO 22301, the business continuity management systems standard that every resilience programme is measured against.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
An organisation that needs a business continuity management system has no reference in the corpus for what ISO 22301 actually requires, what it is scoped to, or how it differs from the guidance standards that surround it. Continuity planning is then improvised, which is exactly the failure mode the standard was written to prevent.

## Root cause
ISO 22301 is the requirements standard of the ISO management-system family for business continuity. It is maintained by ISO/TC 292 and is written as a requirements standard: an organisation certifies against it, and it is assessed against a clause structure rather than being followed for advice.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html

## The "business continuity management system" concept
ISO 22301 requires a documented management system that an organisation plans, establishes, implements, operates, monitors, reviews, maintains and continually improves. The scope is deliberately broad: it covers the management system itself, not any one technical control.

The standard is written around the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle, the same PDCA structure used by ISO 9001 (quality) and ISO/IEC 27001 (information security). An organisation that already runs an ISO management system can usually integrate its continuity activities rather than standing up a parallel framework.

The full text is paywalled by ISO. This document is grounded in the official catalogue entry and the abstract published on it.

## Gotchas
- **This edition has an amendment.** ISO 22301:2019 alone is not the complete current text; Amd 1:2024 changed the climate-action clauses. Cite both.
- **ISO 22301 is a requirements standard, and it is certifiable.** Do not describe it as guidance. If you are looking for the non-certifiable supporting standards, those are ISO 22302 (2019), ISO 22313 (2019), ISO 22318 (2021) and ISO 22320 (2018).
- **The catalogue title is "Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems — Requirements".** Do not shorten it to "business continuity management".
- The standard is currently at stage 90.92 (to be revised). Check the catalogue before citing an edition number as final.
- **It does not cover ICT recovery.** ISO 22301 requires the organisation to plan for continuity; the technical ICT readiness content is in ISO/IEC 27031.
