# ISO/IEC 27014:2020 — Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Governance of information security

**Issue:** The corpus has no document on ISO/IEC 27014, the guidance on governing information security.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
An organisation's board asks how information security is governed and there is no document in the corpus that states what ISO/IEC 27014 covers, who its audience is, or how it differs from general IT governance. The gap is usually filled with the wrong standard.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 27014 provides guidance on concepts, objectives and processes for the governance of information security, by which organisations evaluate, direct, monitor and communicate their information-security-related processes.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/74046.html

## The "governance of information security" concept
The standard is addressed to three audiences:

- the governing body and top management;
- those responsible for evaluating, directing and monitoring an ISMS based on ISO/IEC 27001;
- those responsible for information security management taking place outside the scope of an ISO/IEC 27001-based ISMS, but within the scope of governance.

It applies to organisations of all types and sizes. It focuses on the three types of ISMS organisation described in its Annex B, while remaining usable by others. All references to an ISMS in the document apply to an ISMS based on ISO/IEC 27001.

Edition 2 was published in December 2020, with a corrected version issued in April 2022.

## Gotchas
- **This is information-security governance, not general IT governance.** The official title is "Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Governance of information security". A document that files it under generic IT governance is mis-filing it.
- **The official title carries the "Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection —" prefix**, which was introduced in the ISO/IEC 27000-family renaming. Older catalogues list it as "Information technology — Security techniques — Governance of information security".
- **Edition 2 (2020) supersedes 2013.** ISO/IEC 27014:2013 is Edition 1 and is withdrawn.
- It is guidance, and it is not certifiable. It supports an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS rather than replacing it.
- It is currently at stage 90.92, meaning a revision is expected. Check the catalogue before asserting that 2020 is the final edition.
- It does not prescribe an organisational structure or a reporting line. The governance model it describes is deliberately adaptable.
