## The "ethical ontology" concept
IEEE 7007-2021 specifies a set of ontologies with different abstraction levels that contain concepts, definitions, axioms, and use cases that assist in the understanding of ethical aspects of robotics and automation systems. It provides a common vocabulary for discussing ethics in robotics.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7007-2021
- **Title:** IEEE Ontological Standard for Ethically Driven Robotics and Automation Systems
- **Status:** Published (2021)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "ethical ontology" pattern
The standard provides a framework for understanding ethical aspects of robotics through ontologies:
- Defining ethical concepts at different abstraction levels
- Establishing axioms for ethical reasoning in robotics
- Providing use cases for ethical decision-making
- Creating a common vocabulary for ethics discussions
- Supporting interoperability between ethical frameworks

This pattern is critical for:
- Robotic system design and development
- Ethical AI integration in robotics
- Cross-disciplinary ethics communication
- Regulatory compliance for autonomous systems

## Gotchas
- IEEE 7007-2021 is about *ontologies* for ethics, not specific ethical principles or processes.
- The standard applies to all robotics and automation systems, not just AI — though AI-enabled robotics are a primary use case.
- The full standard text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
