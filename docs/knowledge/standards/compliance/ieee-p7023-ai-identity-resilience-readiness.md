## The "human-AI identity resilience" concept
IEEE P7023 defines a framework and diagnostic tool for assessing organizational readiness to adopt Generative AI systems with a focus on human-AI identity resilience and interaction. It provides tools for evaluating how organizations can maintain identity integrity when interacting with generative AI systems.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE P7023 (project, no year)
- **Title:** Standard for Assessing Organizational Readiness for Human-Artificial Intelligence (AI) Identity Resilience and Interaction
- **Status:** Project (P = proposed, under development)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "identity resilience" pattern
The project provides a framework for assessing organizational readiness:
- Defining frameworks for human-AI identity resilience
- Creating diagnostic tools for organizational readiness assessment
- Evaluating the impact of generative AI on human identity
- Establishing guidelines for healthy human-AI interaction
- Documenting readiness assessment results and recommendations

This pattern is critical for:
- Organizational change management for AI adoption
- Human-AI interaction design
- Identity protection and resilience
- Regulatory compliance for AI adoption
