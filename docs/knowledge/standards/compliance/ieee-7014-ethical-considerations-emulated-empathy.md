## The "emulated empathy" concept
IEEE 7014-2024 provides guidance and actions for the ethical development, deployment, or decommission of autonomous and intelligent systems that attempt to emulate empathy. It addresses the ethical implications of systems that simulate human emotional responses.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7014-2024
- **Title:** IEEE Standard for Ethical Considerations in Emulated Empathy in Autonomous and Intelligent Systems
- **Status:** Published (2024)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "empathy emulation" pattern
The standard provides guidance for ethical emulated empathy:
- Defining when and how to emulate empathy in autonomous systems
- Establishing ethical boundaries for empathy simulation
- Evaluating the impact of emulated empathy on users
- Creating guidelines for deployment and decommissioning
- Documenting ethical considerations and decisions

This pattern is critical for:
- Healthcare and therapy AI systems
- Customer service chatbots
- Companion robots and social AI
- Regulatory compliance for emotional AI
