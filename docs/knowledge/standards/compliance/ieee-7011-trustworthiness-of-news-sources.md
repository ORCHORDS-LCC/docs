## The "news trustworthiness" concept
IEEE 7011 defines semi-autonomous processes using standards to create and maintain news provender ratings for purposes of publication. It provides a framework for evaluating and rating the trustworthiness of news sources.

## The "news rating" pattern
The standard provides a framework for rating news source trustworthiness:
- Defining criteria for news provender evaluation
- Establishing semi-autonomous rating processes
- Creating and maintaining news source ratings
- Publishing trustworthiness assessments
- Updating ratings based on new evidence

This pattern is critical for:
- Media literacy and fact-checking
- Information integrity and authenticity
- Public trust in news sources
