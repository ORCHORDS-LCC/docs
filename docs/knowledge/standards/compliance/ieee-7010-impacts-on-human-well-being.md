## The "human well-being impact" concept
IEEE 7010-2020 measures the impact of artificial intelligence or autonomous and intelligent systems (A/IS) on humans. It provides a recommended practice for assessing how A/IS affect human well-being across multiple dimensions.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7010-2020
- **Title:** IEEE Recommended Practice for Assessing the Impact of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems on Human Well-Being
- **Status:** Published (2020)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "well-being assessment" pattern
The standard provides a framework for assessing the impact of A/IS on human well-being:
- Identifying dimensions of human well-being affected by A/IS
- Measuring positive and negative impacts across stakeholders
- Evaluating distributional effects on different population groups
- Documenting well-being assessment results and recommendations
- Iterating on system design based on well-being impact

This pattern is critical for:
- Regulatory compliance (EU AI Act, US Executive Order on AI)
- Social impact assessments
- Corporate social responsibility
- Public trust and acceptance

## Verification
The IEEE AIS standards page at https://standards.ieee.org/initiatives/autonomous-intelligence-systems/standards/ lists IEEE 7010-2020 as a published standard.

## Gotchas
- IEEE 7010-2020 is about *human well-being impact*, not transparency (that's 7001) or bias (that's 7003).
- The standard applies to all A/IS, not just AI — though AI/ML systems are a primary use case.
- The full standard text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
