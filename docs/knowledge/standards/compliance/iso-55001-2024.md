# ISO 55001:2024 — Asset management — Asset management system — Requirements

**Issue:** The corpus has no document on ISO 55001, the asset management system requirements standard, and no document that records that the 2014 edition was withdrawn.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
An organisation that runs an asset management system has no reference in the corpus for what ISO 55001 requires, which edition is current, or how the requirements standard relates to the guidance standard that supports it. Asset management is then described in terms the standard does not use, and the withdrawn 2014 edition keeps being cited.

## Root cause
ISO 55001 is the requirements standard of the ISO 55000 asset management series. It specifies the requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and improving an asset management system, and it builds on the principles set out in ISO 55000.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/83054.html

## The "asset management system" concept
The standard covers the full lifecycle of an asset management system: establishing it, implementing it, maintaining it and improving it. The key elements ISO describes are an asset management policy, asset management objectives, the design and implementation of the management system, and continual improvement driven by periodic review.

Two boundaries are worth stating, because they are where this standard is most often over-read:

- **It is scoped to the management system, not to any asset type.** ISO 55001 applies to an organisation that relies on assets to meet its objectives, whatever those assets are. The question of how to manage a particular class of asset is a separate matter.
- **It is a requirements standard, and the sibling standard is guidance.** ISO 55002 gives guidelines for applying an asset management system in accordance with ISO 55001's requirements. Requirements and guidance are not interchangeable, and only one of the pair is certifiable.

The standard is maintained by ISO/TC 251, the technical committee for asset management, which also maintains ISO 55000 and ISO 55002.

## Verification
- ISO 55001:2024 — Published, Edition 2, 2024-07, 18 pages, ISO/TC 251, ICS 03.100.01 and 03.100.70. Catalogue page: `https://www.iso.org/standard/83054.html`
- Current stage: 60.60 — International Standard published.
- ISO 55001:2014 — Withdrawn, Edition 1, 2014-01, 14 pages, ISO/TC 251. Stage 95.99, withdrawal 2024-07-03. Catalogue page: `https://www.iso.org/standard/55089.html`
- ISO 55002:2018 — Published, Edition 2, 2018-11, 72 pages, stage 90.92 (to be revised); the 2020-06 corrected version applies to the Spanish language only. Catalogue page: `https://www.iso.org/standard/70402.html`

The full text is paywalled by ISO. This document is grounded in the official catalogue entries and the abstracts published on them.

## Gotchas
- **ISO 55001:2014 is withdrawn.** It was withdrawn on 2024-07-03, the same day the 2024 edition was published. A citation of the 2014 edition without noting the withdrawal is out of date, and its title was different: "Asset management — Management systems — Requirements".
- **The title changed with the edition.** The current title is "Asset management — Asset management system — Requirements". Note the doubled "management system"; it is not a typo.
- **Do not cite ISO 55002 as if it were a requirements standard.** ISO 55002:2018 is guidelines for applying ISO 55001, and it is not certifiable. ISO 55001 is the requirements standard.
- **ISO 55002:2018 is at stage 90.92**, meaning a revision is expected. Check the catalogue before asserting that 2018 is final.
- **The 2020-06 corrected version of ISO 55002 applies to the Spanish language only.** The English and French versions are not concerned by it.
- This standard does not prescribe a fixed asset register format, a valuation method, or a maintenance interval. Those are local decisions the management system has to make.
