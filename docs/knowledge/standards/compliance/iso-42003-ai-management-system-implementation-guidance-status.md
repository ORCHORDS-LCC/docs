# ISO/IEC 42003 — Lifecycle and Revision Status

**Issue:** ISO/IEC 42003 is at stage 20.00 (Preparatory) as an AWI — no publication date, no page count, no purchase link. Its lifecycle status is the primary information available.
**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
Organisations planning to implement ISO/IEC 42001:2023 need to know whether supporting guidance (ISO/IEC 42003) is available. The answer is: it is under development, at the earliest post-registration stage.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 42003 is listed in the official ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 catalogue (https://committee.iso.org/committee/6794475/x/catalogue/) at stage 20.00 (Preparatory) with status "Under development." No further lifecycle details are published.

**Source:** ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 catalogue:
https://committee.iso.org/committee/6794475/x/catalogue/

## The "stage 20.00" concept
Stage 20.00 (Preparatory) means the project has been registered in the TC/SC work programme but no draft has been produced. This is the stage immediately after project approval (stage 10.99).

For comparison, ISO/IEC 42001:2023's lifecycle (from its catalogue page at https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html):
- 2020-08-26: Project approved (10.99)
- 2020-08-26: Registered in work programme (20.00)
- 2021-08-13: Committee draft (CD) registered (30.00)
- 2022-09-12: DIS registered (40.00)
- 2023-06-01: FDIS approved (40.99)
- 2023-12-02: International Standard under publication (60.00)
- 2023-12-18: International Standard published (60.60)

Total: ~3 years 4 months from proposal to publication.

## The "AWI" pattern
ISO/IEC 42003 holds the status "AWI" (Approved Work Item) at stage 20.00. This means:
- The project has been formally approved by the technical committee.
- No committee draft (CD) has been produced yet.
- No public enquiry (DIS/FDIS stage) has occurred.
- No publication date is set.

## Verification
The ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 catalogue at https://committee.iso.org/committee/6794475/x/catalogue/ lists ISO/IEC 42003 at stage 20.00 (Preparatory) with status "Under development." The ISO/IEC 42001 catalogue page (https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html) confirms the full lifecycle stages for comparison.

## Gotchas
- Stage 20.00 is the earliest stage after project registration. The standard could take 3+ years to publish (based on 42001's timeline).
- There is no catalogue page with a price for 42003 because it does not yet exist as a purchasable document.
- Do not reference ISO/IEC 42003 as a published standard in any compliance evidence. It is an AWI at stage 20.00.
