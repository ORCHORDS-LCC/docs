# ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — Scope and Applicability

**Issue:** ISO/IEC 42005:2025 defines the scope of AI system impact assessments but does not prescribe specific assessment methodologies — it provides guidance on what to assess and why.
**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
Organizations implementing ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AIMS) need to understand how ISO/IEC 42005:2025 fits into their governance framework. The catalogue entry defines the scope but does not reveal the full assessment methodology.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 42005:2025 was published in May 2025. Its catalogue entry at https://www.iso.org/standard/42005 defines the scope as providing guidance for organisations conducting AI system impact assessments, focusing on understanding how AI systems and their foreseeable applications may affect individuals, groups, or society at large.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/42005

## The "scope" concept
The catalogue entry states: "ISO/IEC 42005 provides guidance for organisations conducting AI system impact assessments. These assessments focus on understanding how AI systems — and their foreseeable applications — may affect individuals, groups, or society at large."

Key scope elements from the catalogue:
- **Applicability:** Organisations of any size involved in developing, providing, or using AI-based products or services.
- **Focus:** Understanding foreseeable effects of AI systems on individuals, groups, and society.
- **Relationship to 42001:** Companion standard to ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (management system).
- **Package positioning:** Sold as part of the "Responsible AI governance and impact standards package" with ISO/IEC 42001.

## The "impact assessment" pattern
Unlike ISO/IEC 42001 (which is a management system standard using Plan-Do-Check-Act), ISO/IEC 42005 is specifically about impact assessment — understanding the effects of AI systems on stakeholders. This is a different concern from governance: 42001 tells you how to manage AI responsibly; 42005 tells you how to assess the impact of specific AI systems.

The catalogue entry positions 42005 as the "impact" half of the package:
- ISO/IEC 42001: Management system (governance, policies, procedures)
- ISO/IEC 42005: Impact assessment (effects on individuals, groups, society)

## Verification
The catalogue page at https://www.iso.org/standard/42005 returns HTTP 200 with scope statement, status (Published), edition (1), date (2025-05), page count (39), TC (ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42), and ICS codes. The full clause text is not publicly retrievable (CHF 181 purchase required).

## Gotchas
- The scope statement is brief in the catalogue entry — the full standard (39 pages) likely contains detailed assessment methodologies not available here.
- "Foreseeable applications" is a key phrase — the standard covers not just current use but anticipated future uses of AI systems.
- The standard is part of a package with 42001; implementing both together yields the intended benefit.
