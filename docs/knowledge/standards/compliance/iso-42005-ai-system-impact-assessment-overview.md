# ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI System Impact Assessment Overview

**Issue:** The corpus contains zero documents on ISO/IEC 42005:2025, the first international standard dedicated to AI system impact assessment.
**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
No existing compliance document addresses ISO/IEC 42005:2025 ("Information technology — Artificial intelligence — System impact assessment"). The corpus covers ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AIMS management system) but not its companion standard for impact assessment.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 42005:2025 was published in May 2025 (edition 1, 39 pages) — it is the newest of the ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 family. The corpus was built before its publication.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/42005

## The "AI system impact assessment" concept
ISO/IEC 42005:2025 provides guidance for organisations conducting AI system impact assessments. These assessments focus on understanding how AI systems — and their foreseeable applications — may affect individuals, groups, or society at large.

The standard is part of the "Responsible AI governance and impact standards package" alongside ISO/IEC 42001:2023. Together they form the essential toolkit for ethical, compliant, and trustworthy AI.

Key aspects from the catalogue entry:
- **Reference number:** ISO/IEC 42005:2025
- **Status:** Published
- **Publication date:** 2025-05
- **Edition:** 1
- **Pages:** 39
- **Technical Committee:** ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42
- **ICS codes:** 35.020 (Information technology), 03.100.70 (Artificial intelligence)
- **Price:** CHF 181

## The "Responsible AI governance and impact standards package" pattern
ISO bundles 42001 (management system) and 42005 (impact assessment) as a package with a 10% discount. This reflects the standard's design philosophy: governance (42001) and impact assessment (42005) are complementary, not standalone.

The package is described as: "Build trust in AI — Get the responsible AI governance and impact standards package: ISO/IEC 42001 and ISO/IEC 42005 — your essential toolkit for ethical, compliant, and trustworthy AI."

## Verification
The catalogue page at https://www.iso.org/standard/42005 returns HTTP 200 with scope, status (Published), edition (1), date (2025-05), page count (39), TC (ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42), and ICS codes. The full clause text is not publicly retrievable (CHF 181 purchase required).

## Gotchas
- ISO/IEC 42005 is NOT the same as ISO/IEC 42001. 42001 is the management system standard (AIMS); 42005 is specifically about impact assessment.
- The standard was published in May 2025 — it is very recent. Many corpora will not yet have covered it.
- The package pricing (CHF 181 for 42005, CHF 225 for 42001) means the full text is behind a paywall. This document is grounded in the official catalogue entry only.
