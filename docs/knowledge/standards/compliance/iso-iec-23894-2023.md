# ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Guidance on risk management

**Issue:** The corpus has no document on ISO/IEC 23894, the ISO guidance on managing risk arising from artificial intelligence.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
An organisation is building, buying or deploying AI and has no document in the corpus that states what ISO/IEC 23894 actually covers, how its guidance differs from the AI risk frameworks issued by regulators, or where its scope stops.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 23894 is the first ISO/IEC standard dedicated to AI risk management. It was developed by ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, the subcommittee responsible for artificial intelligence, and it gives guidance on how an organisation that develops, produces, deploys or uses AI can manage AI-specific risk and integrate risk management into its AI activities.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html

## The "AI risk management" concept
The standard's stated purpose is guidance on how organisations that develop, produce, deploy or use products, systems and services using AI can manage risk **specifically related to AI**, and how to integrate risk management into AI-related activities and functions. It describes processes for implementing and integrating that risk management.

Two properties matter for how it is used:

- **It is guidance, not a requirement.** ISO explicitly states that the guidance can be customised to any organisation and its context. There is no certification and no conformity assessment.
- **It is risk-specific, not AI-specific.** It addresses the risk that AI introduces, not the governance of AI as such, and it does not attempt to be an AI ethics document.

The document was published in February 2023 as Edition 1, the only edition so far.

The full text is paywalled by ISO. This document is grounded in the official catalogue entry and the abstract published on it.

## Gotchas
- **The official title carries the full "Information technology — Artificial intelligence —" prefix.** A document that titles it just "AI — Guidance on risk management" is using the catalogue short title, which is not the title of the standard.
- **Do not call it a requirements standard.** ISO/IEC 23894 is guidance; there is nothing to certify against.
- It does not replace the EU AI Act or any other regulatory instrument. Regulatory conformity and this standard are separate obligations, and satisfying the standard does not establish regulatory compliance.
- It is scoped to risk management. The ethics and trustworthiness guidance is a separate set of standards, notably ISO/IEC 42001.
- It is frequently confused with ISO/IEC 27005. ISO/IEC 27005 is the information-security risk management standard; ISO/IEC 23894 is the AI-specific one.
