## The "machine-readable privacy terms" concept
IEEE 7012-2025 identifies and addresses the manner in which personal privacy terms are proffered and how they can be read and agreed to by individuals. It provides a framework for making privacy terms machine-readable and actionable.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7012-2025
- **Title:** Standard for Machine Readable Personal Privacy Terms
- **Status:** Published (2025)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "machine-readable privacy" pattern
The standard provides a framework for machine-readable privacy terms:
- Defining structured formats for privacy terms
- Establishing mechanisms for individual consent
- Creating processes for reading and agreeing to terms
- Ensuring terms are both human-readable and machine-processable
- Validating compliance with privacy regulations

This pattern is critical for:
- Regulatory compliance (GDPR, CCPA, PIPL)
- User consent and data protection
- Privacy engineering practices
- Regulatory audit readiness

## Verification
The IEEE AIS standards page at https://standards.ieee.org/initiatives/autonomous-intelligence-systems/standards/ lists IEEE 7012-2025 as a published standard.

## Gotchas
- IEEE 7012-2025 is about *machine-readable privacy terms*, not general data privacy (that's 7002).
- The standard applies to all personal privacy terms, not just AI — though AI/ML systems are a primary use case.
- The full standard text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
