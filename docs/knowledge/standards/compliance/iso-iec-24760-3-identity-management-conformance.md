## Root cause
ISO/IEC 24760-3:2016 "provides guidance for the management of identity information and
for ensuring that an identity management system conforms to ISO/IEC 24760-1 and
ISO/IEC 24760-2." Published 2016-08, 31 pages.

**Source:** ISO catalogue:
`https://www.iso.org/standard/57916.html`

## The "conformance" concept
ISO/IEC 24760-3 provides guidance on ensuring identity management systems conform to
the first two parts of the ISO/IEC 24760 series:

- **Conformance assessment** — how to determine whether an identity management system
  conforms to ISO/IEC 24760-1 and 24760-2 (VERIFIED from catalogue scope)
- **Identity information management** — guidance for managing identity information
  within a conformant system (VERIFIED)
- **Management procedures** — operational guidance for maintaining conformance (VERIFIED)

The standard applies to organizations assessing or maintaining conformance of identity
management systems.

## The "conformance assessment" pattern
ISO/IEC 24760-3 structures conformance guidance around:

1. **Assessment preparation** — scope definition, criteria selection, resource
   allocation
2. **Conformance evaluation** — comparing system behavior against ISO/IEC 24760-1
   and 24760-2 requirements
3. **Gap identification** — identifying areas of non-conformance
4. **Remediation guidance** — recommendations for achieving conformance

This pattern is VERIFIED from the catalogue scope. The standard provides guidance,
not requirements; it does not mandate specific assessment methodologies.

## Gotchas
- ISO/IEC 24760 is a multi-part standard. Part 3 covers conformance guidance; it does
  not define terms (Part 1) or implementation requirements (Part 2). This document
  covers Part 3 only.
- The standard provides *guidance* for conformance, not a conformance test suite. It
  does not specify pass/fail criteria for specific implementations.
- The standard was published in 2016 and remains current. No revision has been issued
  as of the catalogue verification date.
