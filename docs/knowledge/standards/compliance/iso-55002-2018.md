# ISO 55002:2018 — Asset management — Management systems — Guidelines

## Issue

The corpus has no document on ISO 55002, the guideline standard that explains how to apply the requirements of ISO 55001 in practice.

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom

An organisation has adopted or is considering ISO 55001 for asset management but has no reference document describing ISO 55002, the companion standard that provides detailed guidance on implementing the requirements specified in ISO 55001.

## Root cause

ISO 55002:2018 provides guidelines for the application of ISO 55001, the requirements standard for asset management management systems. It is intended to assist organisations — whether or not they intend to pursue certification against ISO 55001 — in understanding and implementing the requirements of ISO 55001 effectively. The standard covers all clauses of ISO 55001 and provides practical guidance, examples and explanations for each requirement.

Edition 2 was published in November 2018, replacing the withdrawn ISO 55002:2014. The standard is currently in stage 90.92 (to be revised), meaning a future revision is planned but none has been published yet.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/70402.html

## The "asset management guidelines" concept

ISO 55002:2018 is titled "Asset management — Management systems — Guidelines for the application of ISO 55001." It is a guidance standard, not a requirements standard, and therefore cannot be used for certification purposes. Its purpose is to explain the "how" behind each requirement in ISO 55001, providing context, examples and practical advice for implementation.

The standard follows the same clause structure as ISO 55001, enabling direct cross-referencing. It covers topics including: establishing the asset management system context, developing asset management policies and objectives, implementing risk-based decision-making, managing asset data and information, conducting asset performance evaluations, and driving continual improvement.

A key feature of ISO 55002:2018 is its emphasis on **value realisation from assets** — the standard requires organisations to define what value means for their specific context and then align asset management objectives to deliver that value. This goes beyond simple reliability or availability metrics to encompass financial, operational, social and environmental dimensions of asset value.

The 2018 edition (Edition 2) incorporated lessons learned from the implementation of Edition 1, with clearer guidance on risk-based thinking, asset data management, and the integration of asset management with broader organisational strategic planning.

## Verification

- ISO 55002:2018 — Published, Edition 2, 2018-11, 72 pages. Catalogue page: `https://www.iso.org/standard/70402.html`
- Stage 90.92 — International Standard to be revised. A future revision (ISO/CD 55002, Edition 3) is under development.
- Technical Committee: ISO/TC 251 (Asset management).

The standard was previously in effect as ISO 55002:2014 (Edition 1), which was withdrawn when the 2018 edition was published.

## Gotchas

- **Stage 90.92 (to be revised)** does not mean withdrawn — it means a revision is planned. The standard remains valid and usable as-is.
- **ISO 55002 is a guidance standard, not certifiable.** Only ISO 55001 (the requirements standard) can be used for certification. ISO 55002 exists solely to help organisations implement ISO 55001 effectively.
- **ISO 55002:2014 (Edition 1) was withdrawn.** The current valid edition is ISO 55002:2018 (Edition 2). Do not cite the 2014 edition for current work.
- **A third edition is under development.** ISO/CD 55002 (Edition 3) is currently in the committee draft consultation phase. When published, it will replace ISO 55002:2018.
- **It is companion-only to ISO 55001.** ISO 55002 does not stand alone — it exists solely to explain ISO 55001's requirements. There is no independent "ISO 55002 requirements" standard.
