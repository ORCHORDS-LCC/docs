# ISO/IEC 27031:2025 — Cybersecurity — Information and communication technology readiness for business continuity

**Issue:** The corpus has no document on ISO/IEC 27031, the guidance on making ICT ready to support business continuity.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
An organisation has a business continuity plan and a security programme but no reference in the corpus for the ICT-specific readiness work that sits between them — no document states what ISO/IEC 27031 covers, or how its guidance differs from the business continuity management standard itself.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 27031 provides guidance on ensuring that information and communication technology is prepared to support business continuity. It outlines a framework for ICT readiness aligned with broader business continuity objectives, helping organisations prevent, respond to and recover from ICT-related disruptions that could affect critical operations.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/27031

## The "ICT readiness" concept
ISO/IEC 27031 addresses ICT readiness in the context of information security. It integrates readiness planning into business continuity and information security practices so that ICT services can be restored within agreed timeframes, and it explicitly extends to **dependencies on third-party services such as cloud providers** — the point at which most continuity failures actually occur.

Its relationship to the neighbouring standards is worth stating plainly, because the standard's own material does: it complements ISO/IEC 27001 by addressing ICT continuity in the context of information security, and it supports ISO 22301 by aligning ICT readiness with broader business continuity objectives.

Edition 2 was published in May 2025, replacing the 2011 first edition, which ISO states was withdrawn.

## Gotchas
- **ISO/IEC 27031:2011 is withdrawn.** The current published edition is 27031:2025. A citation of the 2011 edition without noting its withdrawal is out of date.
- **The official title changed.** Edition 2 is titled "Cybersecurity — Information and communication technology readiness for business continuity". The 2011 edition was titled "Information technology — Security techniques — Guidelines for ICT readiness for business continuity". Do not carry the old title onto the new edition.
- **It is guidance, not requirements**, and it is not certifiable. It works alongside ISO 22301, which is the certifiable business continuity standard; it does not replace it.
- It is scoped to ICT **readiness**. It does not specify recovery technology, backup media, or DR architecture, and it does not cover business continuity management as a whole.
- It is a separate standard from ISO/IEC 27002. It is about continuity of the ICT service, not about the selection of security controls.
- The standard explicitly takes account of reliance on external services such as cloud platforms. A readiness plan that assumes in-house infrastructure only does not meet it.
