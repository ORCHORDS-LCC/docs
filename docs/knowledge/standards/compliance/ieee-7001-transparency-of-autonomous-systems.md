## The "transparency levels" concept
IEEE 7001-2021 describes measurable, testable levels of transparency, so that autonomous systems can be objectively assessed, and levels of compliance determined. It provides a framework for evaluating how transparent an autonomous system is to its users and stakeholders.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7001-2021
- **Title:** IEEE Standard for Transparency of Autonomous Systems
- **Status:** Published (2021)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "transparency assessment" pattern
The standard provides a framework for assessing transparency across multiple dimensions:
- Defining measurable transparency levels
- Establishing testable criteria for compliance
- Evaluating system behavior from user perspective
- Documenting transparency features and limitations
- Iterating on design based on transparency assessment

This pattern is critical for:
- Regulatory compliance (EU AI Act transparency requirements)
- User trust and accountability
- Auditability and explainability
- Public safety and oversight

## Verification
The IEEE AIS standards page at https://standards.ieee.org/initiatives/autonomous-intelligence-systems/standards/ lists IEEE 7001-2021 as a published standard.

## Gotchas
- IEEE 7001-2021 is about *transparency* specifically, not bias (that's 7003) or well-being (that's 7010).
- The standard applies to all autonomous systems, not just AI — though AI/ML systems are a primary use case.
- The full standard text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
