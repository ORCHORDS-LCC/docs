## The "fail-safe design" concept
IEEE 7009-2024 establishes a practical, technical baseline of specific methodologies and tools for the development, implementation, and use of effective fail-safe designs in autonomous and semi-autonomous systems. It provides guidance on ensuring systems fail safely when malfunctions occur.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7009-2024
- **Title:** IEEE Standard for Fail-Safe Design of Autonomous and Semi-Autonomous Systems
- **Status:** Published (2024)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "fail-safe engineering" pattern
The standard provides methodologies for fail-safe design:
- Identifying potential failure modes in autonomous systems
- Designing fallback mechanisms for safe operation
- Implementing monitoring and detection of malfunctions
- Creating graceful degradation strategies
- Validating fail-safe behavior through testing

This pattern is critical for:
- Safety-critical autonomous systems (vehicles, medical devices)
- Regulatory compliance for safety standards
- Risk mitigation and liability reduction
- Public safety and trust
