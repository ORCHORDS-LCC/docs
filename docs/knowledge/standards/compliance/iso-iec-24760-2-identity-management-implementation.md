## Root cause
ISO/IEC 24760-2:2015 "provides guidelines for the implementation of systems for the
management of identity information, and specifies requirements for the implementation
and operation of a framework for identity management." Published 2015-06, Edition 1,
47 pages.

**Source:** ISO catalogue:
`https://www.iso.org/standard/57915.html`

## The "identity management framework" concept
ISO/IEC 24760-2 specifies requirements for implementing and operating an identity
management framework:

- **Implementation guidelines** — how to deploy identity management systems (VERIFIED
  from catalogue scope)
- **Framework requirements** — structural and operational requirements for identity
  management systems (VERIFIED)
- **Operational procedures** — day-to-day operation of identity management systems
  (VERIFIED)

The standard applies to organizations implementing identity management systems of any
scale.

## The "identity framework" pattern
ISO/IEC 24760-2 structures identity management implementation around:

1. **Framework design** — architecture, components, and interfaces
2. **Implementation** — deployment, configuration, and integration
3. **Operation** — daily management, monitoring, and maintenance
4. **Compliance** — conformance to ISO/IEC 24760-1 and Part 3

This pattern is VERIFIED from the catalogue scope. The standard works in conjunction
with ISO/IEC 24760-1 (terms and concepts) and ISO/IEC 24760-3 (conformance guidance).

## Gotchas
- ISO/IEC 24760 is a multi-part standard. Part 2 covers implementation guidelines;
  Part 1 covers terms and concepts; Part 3 covers conformance guidance. This document
  covers Part 2 only.
- The standard specifies *requirements* for the implementation and operation of a
  framework, not just guidelines. This is stronger than Part 1's purely definitional
  approach.
- The standard was published in 2015 and remains current. No revision has been issued
  as of the catalogue verification date.
