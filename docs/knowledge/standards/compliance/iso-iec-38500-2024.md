# ISO/IEC 38500:2024 — Information technology — Governance of IT for the organization

## Issue

The corpus has no document on ISO/IEC 38500, the International Standard for governance of information technology within organisations.

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom

An organisation's IT governance framework lacks a reference to ISO/IEC 38500, the standard that defines the responsibilities and processes for senior management to evaluate, direct and monitor IT use. Without this reference, governance decisions about technology investments, risk acceptance and performance measurement are made without a recognised framework.

## Root cause

ISO/IEC 38500:2024 specifies the model, terms and definitions for the governance of information technology (IT) within an organisation. It is intended for use by senior management, board members, IT managers and anyone responsible for governance of IT. The standard defines six key processes: Evaluate, Direct and Monitor (EDM), covering IT acquisition, performance, risk, compliance, human behaviour and resource responsibility.

Edition 3 was published in February 2024, replacing ISO/IEC 38500:2008 (Edition 2) and ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 (Edition 1) in part. The standard is currently in stage 60.60 (published), meaning it is the current valid version with no planned revision.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/81684.html

## The "governance of IT" concept

ISO/IEC 38500:2024 is titled "Information technology — Governance of IT for the organization." It provides a framework for senior management to govern the use of information technology within their organisation. The standard is structured around six processes:

- **Evaluate** — Assess current and proposed IT resources and processes.
- **Direct** — Provide instructions for IT resource use and process execution.
- **Monitor** — Ensure performance of IT resources and processes against policies, standards and requirements.

The standard applies to all organisations regardless of size, type or nature, and covers all forms of IT use — whether delivered internally, through outsourcing, or as a service. It is deliberately technology-agnostic, focusing on governance principles rather than technical implementation details.

Edition 3 (2024) updated the standard to reflect changes in the IT landscape, including cloud computing, digital transformation, cybersecurity governance, and the increasing importance of data governance. The standard now includes more explicit guidance on emerging technology governance, including artificial intelligence and data analytics.

## Verification

- ISO/IEC 38500:2024 — Published, Edition 3, 2024-02, 21 pages. Catalogue page: `https://www.iso.org/standard/81684.html`
- Stage 60.60 — International Standard published. No planned revision.
- Technical Committee: ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 40 (IT service management and IT governance). ICS: 35.020 (Information technology (IT) in general).

The standard replaced ISO/IEC 38500:2008 (Edition 2) and incorporated elements from ISO/IEC 19770-1:2017 (Edition 1) related to IT governance.

## Gotchas

- **Edition 3 is the current version.** ISO/IEC 38500:2008 (Edition 2) is withdrawn. Do not cite the 2008 edition for current work.
- **It is a governance standard, not an implementation standard.** ISO/IEC 38500 defines what senior management should do to govern IT, not how to implement specific IT systems. It does not replace ISO/IEC 20000 (IT service management) or ISO/IEC 27001 (information security).
- **It applies to all forms of IT use.** The standard covers internally delivered IT, outsourced IT, and IT as a service. It is not limited to any specific technology or delivery model.
- **The standard is deliberately technology-agnostic.** It does not prescribe specific technologies, architectures or tools. It focuses on governance principles and processes that apply regardless of the technology being governed.
- **It is not certifiable.** ISO/IEC 38500 is a guidance standard, not a requirements standard. It cannot be used for certification purposes.
