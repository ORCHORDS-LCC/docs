# ISO/IEC 27010:2015 — Information technology — Security techniques — Information security management for inter-sector and inter-organizational communications

**Issue:** The corpus has no document on ISO/IEC 27010, the guidance for running information security management across organisational and sector boundaries.
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom
An organisation shares sensitive information with a partner, a sector body, or a public authority and has no reference in the corpus for the guidance that governs such exchanges, what it requires to be established before sharing, and how the two parties agree responsibilities.

## Root cause
ISO/IEC 27010 provides guidelines, in addition to the guidance of the ISO/IEC 27000 family, for implementing information security management **within information sharing communities**. It gives controls and guidance for initiating, implementing, maintaining and improving information security in inter-organizational and inter-sector communications, and sets out general principles for meeting the requirements using established messaging and other technical methods.

**Source:** ISO catalogue entry:
https://www.iso.org/standard/68427.html

## The "information sharing community" concept
The standard is written for the case where the organisation does not control the whole exchange. Its applicability covers all forms of exchange and sharing of sensitive information, public and private, national and international, within a market sector or between sectors — including exchanges relating to the provision, maintenance and protection of critical infrastructure.

The stated purpose is to support the creation of trust when exchanging and sharing information, which is why the standard's emphasis is on the governance of a shared arrangement (who agrees what, and how it is reviewed) rather than on the protection of a single system.

## Gotchas
- **There is no ISO/IEC 27010:2022.** The published editions are 27010:2012 (Edition 1) and 27010:2015 (Edition 2). A citation of 27010:2022 cannot be verified in the ISO catalogue and should not be repeated.
- **The official title is "Information technology — Security techniques — Information security management for inter-sector and inter-organizational communications".** The "Security techniques" element was dropped from ISO/IEC 27000-family titles in later editions and is easy to omit by mistake; for this edition it is part of the title.
- It is **guidelines**, not requirements. It supplements the ISO/IEC 27000 family rather than adding mandatory controls of its own.
- It is not a data-sharing agreement template. It does not supply contractual clauses, and it does not replace legal agreements between the parties.
- The standard's scope is cross-boundary. It is not the place to look for controls protecting a single organisation's internal systems; that is ISO/IEC 27002.
