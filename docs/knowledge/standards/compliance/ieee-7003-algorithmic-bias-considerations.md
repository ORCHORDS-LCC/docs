## The "algorithmic bias" concept
IEEE 7003-2024 describes processes and methodologies to help users address issues of bias in the creation of algorithms. It provides practical guidance for identifying, assessing, and mitigating bias throughout the algorithm development lifecycle.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7003-2024
- **Title:** IEEE Standard for Algorithmic Bias Considerations
- **Status:** Published (2024)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "bias mitigation" pattern
Algorithmic bias mitigation involves:
- Identifying potential sources of bias in training data and algorithm design
- Assessing the impact of bias on different stakeholder groups
- Implementing mitigation strategies throughout the algorithm lifecycle
- Evaluating deployed algorithms for emergent bias
- Documenting bias assessment results and mitigation decisions

This pattern is critical for:
- Regulatory compliance (EU AI Act, US Executive Order on AI)
- Fairness and non-discrimination requirements
- User trust and social responsibility
- Auditability and accountability

## Gotchas
- IEEE 7003-2024 is the *algorithmic bias* standard (not 7002, which is about data privacy process).
- The standard applies to algorithms broadly, not just AI — though AI/ML is the primary use case.
- The full standard text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
