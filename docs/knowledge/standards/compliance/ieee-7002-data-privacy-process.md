## Root cause

IEEE 7002-2022 is a published standard in the IEEE 7000 series, which addresses ethical and privacy concerns in technology design. The corpus focuses on ISO/IEC standards, NIST frameworks, and regulatory compliance, so IEEE's privacy-process standards are not yet represented.

## The "data privacy process" concept
IEEE 7002-2022 establishes the requirements for a systems/software engineering process for privacy-oriented considerations regarding products, services, and systems. It provides a framework for integrating privacy considerations into the system development lifecycle.

Key aspects from the IEEE catalogue:
- **Standard number:** IEEE 7002-2022
- **Title:** IEEE Standard for Data Privacy Process
- **Status:** Published (2022)
- **Family:** IEEE 7000 series (AI ethics, transparency, bias)

## The "privacy-by-design" pattern
The standard provides a process for embedding privacy considerations throughout system development:
- Identifying privacy requirements early in the design phase
- Integrating privacy controls into system architecture
- Evaluating privacy risks throughout the lifecycle
- Documenting privacy decisions and trade-offs
- Validating privacy implementation through testing

This pattern is critical for:
- Regulatory compliance (GDPR, CCPA, PIPL)
- Data protection impact assessments
- Privacy engineering practices
- Regulatory audit readiness

## Verification
The IEEE AIS standards page at https://standards.ieee.org/initiatives/autonomous-intelligence-systems/standards/ lists IEEE 7002-2022 as a published standard.

## Gotchas
- IEEE 7002-2022 is about *data privacy process*, not algorithmic bias (that's 7003-2024).
- The standard applies to all systems and products, not just AI — though AI/ML systems are a primary use case.
- The full standard text is behind IEEE's paywall (typically $95-$100 for individual standards).
