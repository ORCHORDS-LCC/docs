# Organizational leadership is responsible and accountable for cybersecurity risk and fosters a culture that is risk-aware, ethical, and continually improving Outcome Governance Profile

## Authoritative outcome

**GV.RR-01:** Organizational leadership is responsible and accountable for cybersecurity risk and fosters a culture that is risk-aware, ethical, and continually improving

## Outcome context

- Function: GV — GOVERN
- Category: GV.RR — Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities

## Governance pattern

1. Treat the outcome as an explicit review question rather than assuming it is satisfied by tooling or policy language.
2. Define the real scope before judging the outcome.
3. Inspect current technical, process, governance, or operational evidence.
4. Separate documented intent from verified behavior.
5. Record partial coverage and unresolved gaps instead of converting them into a pass.
6. Revisit the outcome when the underlying scope, threat environment, technology, suppliers, or mission materially changes.

## Evidence expectations

- a named owner for the outcome and the scope it covers;
- current evidence showing the outcome is met, dated and attributable;
- a record of partial coverage where only part of the scope is addressed;
- an open gap list with owners and remediation dates.

## Boundary

This profile is derived from the stated outcome. It does not invent an incident, prescribe one implementation, or claim the outcome has been achieved.
