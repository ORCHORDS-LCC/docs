# 03.10.05 Protection Governance

## Reference

- Control: **SP_800_171_03.10.05 — 03.10.05**
- Family: SP_800_171_03.10

## Control statement



## Governance pattern

1. Confirm the control applies to the systems in scope, and record the applicability decision.
2. Resolve the organization-defined parameters before evaluating the requirement.
3. Identify the protected asset or process the control operates on, and who is accountable for it.
4. Record the decision on how the control will be met, and the reasoning behind it.
5. Record the evidence that would show the control operating over time, not only that a policy exists.
6. Record the supplier or inherited responsibility where the protection is shared.
7. Review when the catalog is revised or when the protected asset changes materially.

## Controls and evidence

- the resolved statement and parameters, dated and attributable;
- the decision record and the named owner;
- design and configuration evidence for the protected asset;
- operational records showing the control operating;
- supplier evidence where the protection is inherited;
- a documented exception with a compensating control and an expiry date.

## Limitations

A catalog statement describes what a control requires in general terms. It does not establish that any particular implementation satisfies it, and it does not replace the assessment evidence.
