# An Agreement between the Internet Society and Sun Microsystems, Inc. in the Matter of ONC RPC and XDR Protocols Risk Ownership Review

## Reference

- Document: **RFC 1790 — An Agreement between the Internet Society and Sun Microsystems, Inc. in the Matter of ONC RPC and XDR Protocols**
- Series status: INFORMATIONAL

## Risk ownership review

1. Record which business capability depends on the document's subject matter.
2. Record the accountable owner for that capability, and their acceptance of the risk.
3. Record the operational consequence of the document not being implemented, in terms of that capability.
4. Record the decision: implement, defer with a compensating control, or decline, and the reason.
5. Record the review date for a deferred decision, and the trigger that would force an earlier review.
6. Record how the decision is communicated to the people who would operate the capability.

## Ownership record

- Capability affected: _to be completed_
- Accountable owner: _to be completed_
- Decision: _to be completed_
- Deferral expiry or review trigger: _to be completed_

## Limitations

This review records the ownership decision and its basis. It does not assess technical risk and does not replace a risk assessment.
